Exploring abandoned buildings of Virginia

Abandoned buildings give us a look into the past, but also a glimpse into our future. Despite being completely untouched, they still change. The houses serve as a reminder that though we think things seem stagnant, they are slowly growing and changing over time. To humans, we have outlasted many species, innovated tools that seem almost impossibly advanced, but in the end, we will only be a drop in the ocean of time.
